How to read it. Win chance is the sharp bookmaker's live line (Pinnacle) with its margin removed. Edge is win chance minus what you pay, Jupiter's fee included. Return / $1 is the expected profit per dollar staked. $ available is how much you can buy while the bet stays at zero or positive edge. Bets with no matching sharp line are counted as “no sharp line” and are never shown with a made-up number.

Live prices move fast, so an edge can be gone by the time you click. Check Updated, re-scan, and confirm the market rules (overtime, extra time, retirements) before betting. Edges under about 1% are within the margin of error. Not financial advice. Bet responsibly. 🍀
